About GDG & DevFest

Google Developer Groups (GDGs) are local communities of developers who are interested in Google's developer technology.

DevFests are community-led developer events hosted by Google Developer Groups around the globe. DevFests 2025 is a 3-day event full of sessions, workshops, and networking opportunities.
